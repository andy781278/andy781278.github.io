A live Circle Empires simulation.

The map

  • Empires research faster in teal areas.
  • Empires grow their population faster in gold areas.
  • A circle's size shows its population. Power is population times tech level.

Stability and attrition

  • Stability goes from -5 to +5 and drifts at random. Losing a war lowers it.
  • Below 0, an empire can suffer a rebellion, where a piece splits off as rebels, or a revolution, where it breaks into rival factions.
  • War attrition builds up in battles and lowers battle strength. At 80 or more, an empire cannot start wars.

War

  • Empires that collide may go to war. A red outline and a dashed red line show a war.
  • Each battle is a dice roll weighted by tech, size, stability, and attrition.
  • A much stronger winner can make the loser a puppet. Puppets take their master's color and fight its wars.

Great powers and diplomacy

  • The top 5 empires with at least 10,000 power are great powers. Each has a faint sphere of influence. When two spheres touch, war is likely.
  • Nearby empires form alliances, shown by a colored inner ring and the alliance name. At 0 cohesion the alliance breaks. At 100 its members merge into one empire.
  • If one great power becomes too strong, the other great powers form a coalition and go to war with it.

Events

  • One event happens every 100 to 200 seconds.
  • Plague kills part of an empire's population and spreads to its neighbors.
  • Dark Ages cuts the tech level of every empire in a large area.
  • Pax Romana ends all wars for 10 seconds and doubles population growth.

Controls

  • Hover over an empire to see its stats. Hold left click to see the growth rates at that spot.
  • The arrow in the top left opens the leaderboard. The buttons at the top change the speed.
  • Keys: Q merges the hovered empire's alliance, W starts an uprising in it, E starts a plague, R starts the Dark Ages at the cursor, and T starts Pax Romana.
